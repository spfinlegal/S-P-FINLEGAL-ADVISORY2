Advance Tax in India: Due Dates, Calculation & Avoiding Section 234B/234C Penalties

Authored by Shailesh Shinde (Finance Advisor)
Income Tax Act, 1961 Guide
Section 208, 234B & 234C

Core Compliance Rule

Under Section 208 of the Income Tax Act, 1961, if your estimated total income tax liability for any financial year—after reducing Tax Deducted at Source (TDS)—is ₹10,000 or more, you must pay tax in four quarterly installments. Waiting until July or December to pay during ITR filing triggers mandatory interest penalties under Sections 234B and 234C.

1. Advance Tax Schedule & Percentage Due Dates

For all corporate and non-corporate taxpayers (except presumptive taxpayers under Section 44AD/44ADA), advance tax must be paid in four installments:

Installment Due Date Cumulative Percentage Due Amount to Pay
On or before June 15 15% of estimated net tax First 15% installment
On or before September 15 45% of estimated net tax 45% minus tax paid in 1st installment
On or before December 15 75% of estimated net tax 75% minus tax paid in previous installments
On or before March 15 100% of estimated net tax Remaining balance of total estimated tax

Special Rule for Presumptive Taxpayers (Section 44AD & 44ADA): Taxpayers who opted for the presumptive taxation scheme are allowed to pay 100% of their advance tax in a single installment on or before March 15.

2. Who is Exempt from Advance Tax?

Under Section 207(2), a resident individual who:

  • Is a senior citizen (aged 60 years or above at any time during the financial year), and
  • Does not have any income chargeable under the head "Profits and Gains of Business or Profession" (PGBP).

Such senior citizens can pay their entire tax liability at the time of filing their regular ITR without incurring Section 234B/234C penalties.

3. How Section 234B and Section 234C Interest is Charged

Section 234C (Deferment of Advance Tax Installments)

If you pay less than the required percentage in any quarter (e.g., less than 12% by June 15 or less than 36% by Sept 15), simple interest at 1% per month for 3 months is levied on the shortfall amount for each deferred installment.

Section 234B (Default in Payment of Advance Tax)

If you fail to pay at least 90% of your total assessed tax through advance tax by March 31, simple interest at 1% per month is levied from April 1 of the assessment year until the date you pay the regular tax.

4. Practical Calculation Steps for Small Business Owners

  1. Estimate Total Gross Income: Sum anticipated revenues from business turnover, professional fees, capital gains, interest, and rental income.
  2. Deduct Permissible Business Expenses: Deduct actual operating overheads, depreciation, salaries, and interest.
  3. Compute Gross Tax Liability: Apply applicable slab rates (for individuals/proprietors) or flat corporate rates (22% / 30% for companies and LLPs).
  4. Subtract TDS / TCS Credits: Deduct total TDS already deducted by clients (verified through Form 26AS / AIS).
  5. Pay Challan 280 (Minor Head 100): Generate e-pay tax challans on the Income Tax e-filing portal selecting "Advance Tax (100)".
