Income Tax

Got an Income Tax Notice? What It Means and What To Do Next

Written by Shailesh Shinde
Income Tax Act Advisory Guide

In short

An income tax notice does not always mean you did something wrong. Many are only a mismatch or a request for information. Do not ignore it. Check if it is genuine, read the due date, and reply on the e-filing portal with proof.

1. Do not panic, but do not ignore

Notices are sent for many routine reasons. But if you do not respond within the time given, the Department can make its own assessment and charge extra tax, interest or penalty.

2. Common reasons for notices

  • Intimation under Section 143(1): a computer-generated result after your return is processed. It may show a refund, a demand, or no change.
  • Mismatch between your ITR and AIS or Form 26AS.
  • Defective return under Section 139(9): some detail was missing or wrong.
  • Notice for more information or inquiry (Section 142(1)).
  • Scrutiny notice (Section 143(2)): your return is selected for detailed checking.
  • Notice about unreported income or tax escaped (Section 148).

3. Check that it is genuine

  • Log in to the official e-filing portal yourself (do not click links in emails) and check Pending Actions and e-Proceedings.
  • The Department does not ask for your bank password, PIN or OTP over email or phone.
  • Check the DIN (Document Identification Number) printed on the notice.

4. Steps to respond

  1. Read the notice fully. Note the section, assessment year and last date to reply.
  2. Download your ITR, AIS, Form 26AS and related papers.
  3. Find out the exact reason. Match numbers with your records.
  4. Gather proof: bank statements, invoices, agreements, Form 16, receipts.
  5. Reply on the portal through the response option. Upload clear documents.
  6. If you agree with the demand, pay it. If not, give reasons and proof.
  7. Keep a copy of your reply and the acknowledgement.

5. When should you take professional help?

Take help if you got a scrutiny or reassessment notice, a large demand, or if you are not sure how to answer. A wrong or late reply can make things worse.

Frequently asked questions

Is every income tax notice a sign of tax evasion?

No. Many notices are routine, such as a processing result or a data mismatch.

How do I reply to an income tax notice?

Log in to the e-filing portal, go to e-Proceedings or Pending Actions, open the notice and submit your response with documents before the due date.

What happens if I ignore the notice?

The Department can complete the assessment on its own, which can lead to extra tax, interest and penalty.

This guide is for general information. Laws, rates and due dates change, so please confirm the latest rules or speak to us before you act.
