GST Registration & Monthly Compliance Checklist for Small Businesses in Maharashtra

Authored by Shailesh Shinde (Finance Advisor)
Statutory GST Compliance Guide
Central & Maharashtra GST Acts

Critical Takeaway

Obtaining a GSTIN is merely step one. True GST compliance requires monthly matching between your sales registers (GSTR-1), supplier-filed credits (GSTR-2B), and cash payments (GSTR-3B). Under Section 16(4) of the CGST Act, claiming unmatched Input Tax Credit (ITC) can lead to automated demand notices and blocking of your electronic ledger.

1. Statutory Thresholds for GST Registration in Maharashtra

Under Section 22 of the CGST / Maharashtra GST Act, businesses are required to register for GST once their aggregate pan-India turnover exceeds statutory limits:

Exclusive Supply of Goods
₹40 Lakhs / Year

Applies to traders, wholesalers, manufacturers, and retailers supplying solely within Maharashtra.

Supply of Services & Mixed
₹20 Lakhs / Year

Applies to IT agencies, freelancers, consultants, clinics, and mixed suppliers of goods and services.

2. Compulsory Registration Under Section 24 (Regardless of Turnover)

Even if your turnover is below ₹20 Lakhs or ₹40 Lakhs, registration is legally mandatory under Section 24 in the following circumstances:

  • Persons making any inter-state taxable supply (selling to a customer outside Maharashtra).
  • Casual taxable persons making taxable supply.
  • Persons who are required to pay tax under Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM).
  • Electronic commerce operators (or suppliers selling through platforms such as Amazon, Flipkart, or Swiggy).
  • Non-resident taxable persons making taxable supply.

3. Complete Document Checklist for GST Registration in Pune

Proprietorship:

PAN Card and Aadhaar Card of Proprietor, passport photo, cancelled cheque or latest bank statement, electricity bill of premises, and Rent Agreement / Owner NOC.

Partnership / LLP:

PAN of Firm, Partnership Deed / LLP Agreement, PAN & Aadhaar of all partners, Authorization Letter / Resolution appointing designated partner, bank proof, and office electricity bill.

Private Limited Company:

Certificate of Incorporation, Company PAN, MOA & AOA, Board Resolution authorising signing director, PAN & Aadhaar of directors, bank statement, and registered office electricity bill with rent agreement.

4. The Monthly Filing Rhythm & Strict Deadlines

11th of Every Month (GSTR-1):

Submit outward supplies (all B2B and B2C sales invoices). Your B2B buyers can only claim Input Tax Credit if your GSTR-1 is filed accurately by this date.

14th of Every Month (GSTR-2B Generated):

The GST portal auto-freezes GSTR-2B. This statement determines the maximum allowable Input Tax Credit (ITC) you can claim for the month.

20th of Every Month (GSTR-3B):

Final monthly summary return and payment of net tax liability (Tax on Sales minus eligible ITC). Filing after the 20th triggers a statutory late fee (₹50/day) plus 18% annual interest on the cash portion.
