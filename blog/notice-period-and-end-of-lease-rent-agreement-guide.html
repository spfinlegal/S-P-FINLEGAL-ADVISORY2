Property and Rent

Renewing or Ending a Rent Agreement in Maharashtra: Easy Guide for Owners and Tenants

Written by Adv. Poonam Shinde
Tenancy Law Reference Guide

In short

Read your notice period and lock-in clause first. Renew on time with a fresh registered agreement if the term is going beyond 11 months. At the end, do a joint inspection and take a written receipt for return of the deposit.

1. Know your agreement

Most residential Leave and License agreements in Maharashtra are for 11 months. They usually include a lock-in period, a notice period (often 1 to 2 months), rent amount, increase clause and the security deposit. Read these before you decide anything.

2. Renewing the agreement

  1. Start talking 30 to 45 days before the end date.
  2. Agree in writing on the new rent and any change in terms.
  3. Make a fresh agreement for the new period instead of just a handwritten extension.
  4. Register the agreement and pay stamp duty as required. Tenants should also update police verification details where required.
  5. Keep a signed copy with both parties.
Please note: Stamp duty and registration rules can change. Check the latest rules or ask us for help before signing.

3. Ending the agreement on time

  • Give the notice in writing as per the agreement and keep proof (email, WhatsApp plus signed copy or registered post).
  • Pay rent and bills until the last day.
  • Clear electricity, water, gas and society dues.
  • Return keys, remote controls and any items listed in the inventory.

4. Handover checklist

Take date-stamped photos or videos of the condition on the day of handover.

ItemWho checks
Walls, paint, fittings, sanitaryBoth
Furniture and appliances (as listed)Both
Meter readings for electricity and gasBoth
Society dues and maintenanceOwner and tenant
Keys and access cardsTenant returns, owner confirms

5. Security deposit tips

  • The owner can deduct unpaid rent or bills and the cost of damage beyond normal wear and tear.
  • Ask for the refund in writing with a clear date.
  • If the owner delays or refuses without reason, send a polite written reminder, then a legal notice. Court action is the last step.

6. What if the owner or tenant does not leave?

Do not use force or cut basic services like water or electricity. Follow legal steps: written notice first, then a legal notice and, if needed, approach the competent court. Talk to an advocate before taking any step.

Frequently asked questions

Why are rent agreements usually for 11 months?

Many people choose 11 months to keep things simple. In Maharashtra, Leave and License agreements are generally registered even for short terms, so please follow the registration rules.

How do I get my security deposit back?

Do a joint inspection, clear all dues, return the keys and ask for a written receipt. Follow up in writing if there is a delay.

Can the owner increase the rent at renewal?

Yes, if both agree. The increase should be written in the new agreement.
