How to File Income Tax Return (ITR) in India: Simple Step-by-Step Guide

Written by Shailesh Shinde
Updated: October 2026

In short

Most people with income above the basic exemption limit must file an ITR. The usual last date for individuals who do not need an audit is 31 July. If you file late, you pay a late fee and may lose some benefits. Keep your Form 16, AIS and bank statements ready before you start.

1. What is an Income Tax Return?

An Income Tax Return (ITR) is a yearly form where you tell the Income Tax Department how much you earned and how much tax you have paid. If you paid extra tax, you get a refund. If you paid less, you pay the balance.

2. Who has to file an ITR?

You must file if your total income is above the basic exemption limit for your tax regime. The limit changes with the Budget, so check the current figure for the year you are filing.

  • You have a current account or deposited a large amount of cash in a bank account (limits are set by law).
  • You spent a large amount on foreign travel or paid a high electricity bill (limits are set by law).
  • You are a director in a company or hold unlisted shares.
  • You want to claim a refund of TDS.
  • You are a business owner or professional and want a clean record for loans and visas.

3. Last dates to remember

The Government sometimes extends these dates. Always check the Income Tax portal before the deadline.

WhoUsual last date
Individuals and others who do not need an audit31 July after the financial year ends
Businesses that need a tax audit31 October
Belated return (late filing)31 December
Updated return (to correct or add income)Within 24 months from the end of the assessment year, with extra tax

4. Documents to keep ready

  • PAN card and Aadhaar (they must be linked).
  • Form 16 from your employer (if you are a salaried person).
  • Annual Information Statement (AIS) and Form 26AS from the e-filing portal. These show your income and TDS as the Department sees it.
  • Bank statements and interest certificates.
  • Details of investments for deductions such as 80C and 80D (if you use the old regime).
  • Rent receipts, home loan interest certificate, capital gains statements, and business books if applicable.

5. Which ITR form should you use?

Choosing the wrong form can make your return defective. If you are unsure, ask a tax professional before filing.

FormUsed by
ITR-1Resident individuals with salary, one house property, and other simple income within the limit set by law
ITR-2Individuals with capital gains, more than one house, or foreign income, but no business income
ITR-3Individuals and HUFs with business or professional income
ITR-4Small businesses and professionals using the presumptive scheme (Section 44AD, 44ADA, 44AE)

6. Steps to file online

  1. Log in to the Income Tax e-filing portal with your PAN.
  2. Check your AIS and Form 26AS and match them with your own records.
  3. Select the assessment year and the correct ITR form.
  4. Fill in your income, deductions and tax paid.
  5. Choose between the old and new tax regime. Compare both before choosing.
  6. Pay any balance tax using the e-pay tax option.
  7. Submit and e-verify the return using Aadhaar OTP, net banking or other options. A return is not complete until it is verified.

7. What happens if you file late?

  • A late fee under Section 234F: up to ₹5,000, or up to ₹1,000 if your total income is ₹5 lakh or less.
  • Interest on unpaid tax under Section 234A at 1% per month.
  • You may not be able to carry forward some losses.
  • Banks and visa offices may ask for filed returns, so delays can cause problems.

8. Made a mistake? You can fix it

If you filed on time and found an error, you can file a revised return. If you missed the date, you can file a belated return by 31 December. After that, an updated return (ITR-U) is possible within the time allowed by law, with additional tax.

Frequently asked questions

What is the last date to file ITR for individuals?

It is normally 31 July after the end of the financial year, unless the Government extends it. Always confirm on the Income Tax portal.

What is the late fee for filing ITR after the due date?

Under Section 234F the late fee is up to ₹5,000. It is up to ₹1,000 if your total income is ₹5 lakh or less.

Do I need to file ITR if my employer already cut TDS?

Not always, but filing is needed if you want a refund, if your income is above the limit, or if you have other income such as capital gains.

This guide is for general information. Laws, rates and due dates change, so please confirm the latest rules or speak to us before you act.
