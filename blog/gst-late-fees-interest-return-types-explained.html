GST Returns Explained: GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, Late Fees and Interest in Simple Words

Written by Shailesh Shinde
Updated: October 2026

In short

GSTR-1 shows your sales. GSTR-3B is the summary return where you pay the tax. If you miss the dates you pay a daily late fee and 18% yearly interest on the tax you paid late. Many months of non-filing can lead to cancellation of your registration.

1. The main GST returns in plain words

Businesses under the QRMP scheme file quarterly returns and pay tax monthly using a challan. The dates for them are different, so check the portal.

ReturnWhat it isUsual due date
GSTR-1Details of your sales (outward supplies)11th of the next month (monthly filers)
GSTR-3BSummary of sales, purchases and tax payment20th of the next month (monthly filers)
GSTR-9Yearly return31 December after the financial year
CMP-08 / GSTR-4For composition taxpayersQuarterly / yearly

2. Why do GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B need to match?

Your customers claim Input Tax Credit (ITC) based on your GSTR-1. If you do not file GSTR-1 or file wrong details, your customers cannot claim ITC, which can create disputes. Match both returns every month.

3. Late fee and interest

  • Late fee: ₹50 per day (₹25 CGST plus ₹25 SGST). For nil returns it is ₹20 per day. There is a maximum cap per return which depends on turnover.
  • Interest: 18% per year on the tax amount that is paid late.
  • If you do not file GSTR-3B for a long time (generally six months in a row for regular taxpayers), the officer can start cancellation of your GST number.

4. Monthly checklist

  1. Collect all sales invoices, credit notes and debit notes.
  2. Collect purchase invoices and check them against GSTR-2B.
  3. File GSTR-1 on or before the 11th.
  4. Check ITC available and reverse any ineligible ITC.
  5. Pay the tax and file GSTR-3B on or before the 20th.
  6. Save the filing receipts (ARN) for your records.

5. Common mistakes

  • Claiming ITC on invoices that do not appear in GSTR-2B.
  • Forgetting to file nil returns.
  • Wrong HSN or SAC codes and wrong tax rates.
  • Not reporting advances received.
  • Missing the annual return.

6. Keep your records

Keep your GST records, invoices and returns safe. The law expects you to keep them for a number of years (generally 72 months from the due date of the annual return for that year).

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B?

GSTR-1 gives the details of every sale invoice. GSTR-3B is a summary return in which you claim ITC and pay the tax.

What is the interest rate for late GST payment?

It is 18% per year on the tax that is paid late.

Do I need to file GST returns if I have no sales?

Yes. You must file a nil return on time to avoid late fee and possible cancellation.
