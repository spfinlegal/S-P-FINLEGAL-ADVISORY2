TDS Explained Simply: Deposit Dates, Quarterly Returns and Late Fees

Written by Shailesh Shinde
Income Tax TDS Statutory Reference

In short

TDS means Tax Deducted at Source. If you pay certain amounts (salary, rent, professional fees, contractor payments), you may have to cut tax before paying. You must deposit it with the Government by the 7th of each following month and file quarterly returns. Delays lead to interest and a daily late fee.

1. What is TDS?

TDS is a way of collecting tax at the time money is paid. The person making the payment (the deductor) cuts a small part as tax and deposits it with the Government. The person receiving the money (the deductee) gets credit for it when filing their return.

2. When do you need to deduct TDS?

It depends on the type of payment and the amount. Some common examples are:

  • Salary (Section 192)
  • Payments to contractors (Section 194C)
  • Professional or technical fees (Section 194J)
  • Rent (Section 194-I, and 194-IB for individuals paying high rent)
  • Commission or brokerage (Section 194H)
  • Interest other than on securities (Section 194A)
Please note: Rates and yearly limits for each section are set by law and can change in the Budget. Please confirm the current rate before deducting.

3. When must you deposit the TDS?

  • By the 7th of each following month for deductions made in the prior month.
  • For deductions made in March, the date is 30 April.
  • Deposit using Challan ITNS 281 on the tax portal or through your bank.

4. Quarterly TDS returns

Use Form 24Q for salary TDS and Form 26Q for other payments to residents. After filing, the deductee gets the TDS credit in Form 26AS and AIS.

5. Interest and late fee

  • Interest of 1% per month if TDS is not deducted on time (counted from the date it should have been deducted to the date it is deducted).
  • Interest of 1.5% per month if TDS was deducted but not deposited on time (counted from the date of deduction to the date of deposit).
  • Late fee under Section 234E: ₹200 per day until the return is filed, but not more than the TDS amount for that return.
  • A penalty under Section 271H may apply if the return is not filed within the extended time or has wrong details.

6. Simple tips to avoid trouble

  1. Get a TAN (Tax Deduction Account Number). It is compulsory for deductors.
  2. Collect the PAN of every person you pay. If PAN is missing, the TDS rate can be higher.
  3. Keep a monthly TDS sheet with the payment date, amount, section and rate.
  4. Deposit on time and match your return with the challans.
  5. Download TDS certificates (Form 16 or 16A) from TRACES and give them to the receivers.

Frequently asked questions

What is the recurring due date for depositing TDS?

By the 7th of each following month (with deductions made in March due by 30 April).

What is the late fee for filing a TDS return late?

Under Section 234E it is ₹200 per day until the return is filed, limited to the total TDS for that return.

Which form is used for TDS on salary?

Form 24Q is used for salary TDS. Form 26Q is used for most other payments to residents.
