How to Make a Valid Rent Agreement & Complete Police Verification in Pune (Complete Legal Guide)

Authored by Adv. Poonam Shinde (Notary Advocate)
Verified Legal Guide
Pune & PCMC Jurisdiction

Summary of Key Legal Facts

Under Section 55 of the Maharashtra Rent Control Act, 1999, all tenancy and leave-and-license agreements in Maharashtra must be in writing and registered with the Department of Registration and Stamps (IGR). Notarizing an agreement on ₹100 stamp paper does not satisfy this statutory requirement. Furthermore, orders issued by the Commissioner of Police Pune mandate online tenant reporting under Section 144 CrPC / Section 163 BNSS.

1. Leave and License vs. Lease: Understanding the Legal Basis

In Pune, residential and commercial lettings are almost exclusively executed as Leave and License Agreements rather than leases. This distinction is grounded in Indian law:

  • License (Section 52, Indian Easements Act, 1882): Grants a personal permission to occupy the property for a limited period without transferring any legal interest or tenancy right to the licensee. Upon expiration or lawful termination, the licensee must vacate, making eviction processes far simpler.
  • Lease (Section 105, Transfer of Property Act, 1882): Transfers an interest in the immovable property, creating hereditary tenancy rights and restricting the owner's repossession powers under rent-control protections.

2. Exact Stamp Duty Calculation in Maharashtra (Article 36A)

Stamp duty on Leave and License agreements is governed by Article 36A of the Maharashtra Stamp Act. The computation is strictly prescribed:

Total Consideration = [Monthly Rent × Number of Months] + [10% of Refundable Security Deposit × Number of Years]

Stamp Duty = 0.25% of Total Consideration

Fixed Registration Fee = ₹1,000 for Municipal Corporation limits (PMC and PCMC); ₹500 for Rural areas.

Practical Example: Suppose you rent a 2BHK flat in Wakad or Hinjewadi for 11 months at ₹25,000 per month with a refundable deposit of ₹60,000:

  • Total Rent for 11 months = ₹2,75,000
  • 10% of Deposit for 1 year = ₹6,000
  • Taxable Consideration = ₹2,81,000
  • Stamp Duty (0.25%) = ₹702.50 (rounded to nearest slab, approx. ₹800)
  • Government Registration Fee = ₹1,000
  • Total Government Fees = ₹1,800 + applicable e-portal data handling charges.

3. Essential Clauses Every Landlord and Tenant Must Include

Standard generic templates often omit critical clauses, leading to disputes over deposits or early termination. A properly drafted agreement must clearly address:

Lock-in Period & Notice Period

Specifies the minimum non-breakable duration (e.g., 6 months) and the mandatory written notice period (typically 1 month) required from either party before termination.

Society Maintenance Charges

Explicitly states whether monthly society maintenance is paid by the owner or the licensee to prevent disputes with the Housing Society committee.

Security Deposit Refund & Deductions

Demarcates permitted deductions (unpaid electricity bills, painting costs, actual damages) and establishes the timeline for deposit return upon handing over keys.

Sub-letting & Permitted Use

Strictly prohibits sub-leasing or running commercial or unregistered hostel businesses from a residential premises.

4. Step-by-Step Leave & License E-Registration Process

The Maharashtra Department of Registration and Stamps (IGR) enables digital e-registration via UIDAI biometric authentication. You do not need to stand in long queues at the Sub-Registrar office:

  1. Drafting Terms: Both parties agree on commercial terms (rent, deposit, lock-in, fittings list).
  2. Document Verification: Aadhaar and PAN cards of Licensor, Licensee, and two adult witnesses are verified.
  3. Biometric Fingerprint & Webcam Capture: UIDAI biometric fingerprint authentication and webcam photos are completed for both parties and witnesses.
  4. Submission to IGR Server: The draft and verification tokens are submitted electronically to the Sub-Registrar.
  5. Final Registered Agreement: The Sub-Registrar digitally signs the document. The final registered PDF—complete with official stamp duty challan, unique registration number, and scannable QR code—is generated within 24 to 48 hours.

5. Pune Police Tenant Verification: Official Online Process

Landlords in Pune and Pimpri-Chinchwad must complete police verification under orders promulgated by the Commissioner of Police under Section 144 CrPC / Section 163 of Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS). Failure to comply can result in prosecution under Section 188 of the Indian Penal Code / Section 223 of BNS.

How to Submit Police Verification:

  • Log in to the official Pune Police / PCMC Police Tenant Verification portal.
  • Enter property details, landlord contact information, and tenant employment details (including office address and HR contact).
  • Upload tenant photograph, government identity proof (Aadhaar/Passport), and the registered rent agreement copy.
  • Download the official police acknowledgment receipt and keep a copy for your records.
