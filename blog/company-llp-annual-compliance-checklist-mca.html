Annual Compliance for Pvt Ltd Company and LLP in India: Simple Checklist

Written by Shailesh and Poonam Shinde
Updated: October 2026

In short

Companies and LLPs must file yearly returns with the Registrar of Companies even if there is no business. Delay means additional fees every day, which can become much bigger than the original fee. Make a yearly calendar and follow it.

1. Private Limited Company: yearly tasks

A company must also file its income tax return and maintain statutory registers and minutes.

TaskUsual timeline
Hold the Annual General Meeting (AGM)Within 6 months of the end of the financial year (normally by 30 September)
File financial statements (Form AOC-4)Within 30 days of the AGM
File annual return (Form MGT-7)Within 60 days of the AGM
Appoint or re-appoint auditor (Form ADT-1)Within 15 days of appointment
Director KYC (Form DIR-3 KYC)Every year by 30 September, as required by the rules
Board meetingsAt least 4 per year, with gap not more than 120 days

2. LLP: yearly tasks

Accounts of an LLP need audit only if turnover or contribution crosses the limit set by law.

TaskUsual due date
Form 11 (Annual Return)30 May
Form 8 (Statement of Account and Solvency)30 October
Income Tax Return31 July, or 31 October if audit applies
Partner KYC (DIR-3 KYC), if designated partnersAs per MCA rules

3. What if you miss the date?

  • Additional fees apply for each day of delay on MCA forms. For LLP Form 11 and Form 8 it is ₹100 per day with no upper limit.
  • Directors can be disqualified if financial statements or annual returns are not filed for 3 continuous years.
  • The company can be struck off by the Registrar if it appears inactive.
  • Delays harm your credit profile and make loans and funding harder.

4. A simple yearly calendar

  1. April to June: close books, plan audit, hold board meeting.
  2. July: file income tax return if no audit.
  3. September: finish AGM and director KYC.
  4. October: file AOC-4 and MGT-7; LLP files Form 8.
  5. Throughout the year: keep minutes, registers and GST/TDS filings up to date.

5. Tip

Dates can be extended by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs from time to time. Always check the latest notices on the MCA website.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to file ROC forms if my company has no business?

Yes. A company or LLP must file yearly returns even if there is no turnover.

What is the due date for Form 11 of an LLP?

It is normally 30 May each year.

What is the penalty for late LLP filing?

Additional fee of ₹100 per day for each form until it is filed, with no maximum limit.
