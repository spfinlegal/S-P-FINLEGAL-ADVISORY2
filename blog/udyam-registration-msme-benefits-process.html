Business Registration

Udyam Registration (MSME): Who Can Apply, Benefits and Process

Written by Shailesh Shinde
Ministry of MSME Reference

In short

Udyam Registration is the official government registration for micro, small and medium enterprises. It is online and free of cost on the official Udyam portal. It needs your Aadhaar, PAN and GST details (if any). The certificate can help with bank loans, subsidies and tenders.

1. What is Udyam Registration?

Udyam Registration is the way the Government of India recognises your business as an MSME (Micro, Small or Medium Enterprise). You get a permanent Udyam Registration Number and an e-certificate.

2. Is there any fee?

No. The official registration on udyamregistration.gov.in is free. Be careful of websites that charge a fee for something you can do yourself for free. If you take help from a professional, you are paying only for their time and service.

3. Official Government MSME Classification Criteria

Both investment and turnover limits must be met for the category. Limits can be revised by the Government in future.

TypeInvestment in plant and machinery or equipmentAnnual turnover
MicroUp to ₹2.5 croreUp to ₹10 crore
SmallUp to ₹25 croreUp to ₹100 crore
MediumUp to ₹125 croreUp to ₹500 crore

4. Who can apply?

  • Proprietorships, partnerships, LLPs, companies, HUFs and other business types.
  • Manufacturing, service and trading businesses (as per current rules).
  • One business can have only one Udyam Registration, though it can list multiple activities.

5. What you need

  • Aadhaar number of the owner (or partner or director)
  • PAN of the business or owner
  • GSTIN, if you have one
  • Business name, address, bank details, date of starting, and number of employees
  • Activity code (NIC code) for your business

6. Steps to register

  1. Go to the official Udyam Registration portal.
  2. Enter Aadhaar and verify with OTP.
  3. Enter PAN and verify.
  4. Fill in business details.
  5. Submit and get the Udyam number and certificate by email.

7. Possible benefits

  • Easier access to collateral-free loans under government credit schemes (subject to bank rules).
  • Priority sector lending status with banks.
  • Support in delayed payment disputes under the MSME Samadhaan system.
  • Subsidies and schemes offered by central and state governments.
  • Eligibility for some government tenders and fee concessions.
Please note: Benefits depend on the scheme and the rules at the time. Registration alone does not guarantee a loan or subsidy.

Frequently asked questions

Is Udyam registration free?

Yes. The official registration on the Udyam portal is free of cost.

Do I need GST for Udyam registration?

GST number is needed only if your business is required to have GST. Aadhaar and PAN details are the main requirements.

Does Udyam registration guarantee a loan?

No. It can help you access schemes, but banks still decide based on your business and papers.
